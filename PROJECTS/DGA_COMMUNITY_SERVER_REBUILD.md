## DOCUMENTARY_THESIS

This documentary explores how gaming communities rebuilt abandoned multiplayer infrastructure after official servers shut down.

The video examines:

- digital preservation
- abandoned online systems
- community reconstruction
- fan-operated infrastructure
- multiplayer extinction
- unofficial revival ecosystems

The documentary treats multiplayer communities as historical digital spaces fighting against disappearance.

---

## NARRATIVE_ANGLE

The emotional core is not nostalgia.

The emotional core is:

people refusing to let a digital world disappear.

The documentary should feel:

- investigative
- atmospheric
- slightly melancholic
- historically grounded
- digitally archaeological

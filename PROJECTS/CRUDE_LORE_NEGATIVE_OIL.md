"The Town That Made Oil Worth Negative $37"

---

# DOCUMENTARY THESIS

This documentary explores how oil briefly became so economically broken that producers were paying traders to take barrels away.

The video investigates:

- oil oversupply
- storage collapse
- futures contracts
- infrastructure bottlenecks
- physical delivery systems
- industrial dependency
- logistical panic

The documentary treats the event not as a financial anomaly,
but as a moment where the hidden machinery of industrial civilization became visible.

---

# CORE QUESTION

How can something as globally valuable as oil become worth less than nothing?

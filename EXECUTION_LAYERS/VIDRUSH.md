
# VIDRUSH

## PURPOSE

VIDRUSH.md defines how FacelessOS writes narration for Vidrush-assisted documentary production.

The execution layer exists to preserve:

- retrieval-safe scripting
- visual continuity
- footage match quality
- cinematic pacing
- scene retrievability

while maintaining documentary realism.

The layer bridges:

- narration
- visual retrieval
- AI-assisted footage systems
- documentary pacing

---

## ACTIVATION

Activate VIDRUSH when:

- scripting for Vidrush
- writing footage-first documentaries
- generating AI-assisted B-roll workflows
- building visual-heavy narration
- optimizing retrieval-safe scripting
- creating documentary sequences requiring stock or AI footage matching

## CORE PHILOSOPHY

Vidrush retrieves visuals from narration.

Therefore:

Beautiful narration alone is NOT enough.

Scripts must contain:

- visual nouns
- observable actions
- physical environments
- cinematic transitions
- scene continuity
- retrievable visual language

The goal is not literary writing.

The goal is cinematic documentary scripting.

---

## RETRIEVAL PRINCIPLE

Every major section should contain:

- physical subjects
- observable environments
- visible actions
- environmental movement
- cinematic objects
- retrievable moments

The engine discourages:

- abstract philosophy
- overly conceptual narration
- invisible ideas
- non-visual scripting

---

## VISUAL NOUN SYSTEM

Narration should regularly reference:

- locations
- machinery
- buildings
- vehicles
- weather
- tools
- people
- environments
- systems
- movement

Examples:

GOOD:
"The radar station sat alone above the coastline."

BAD:
"The world was entering a new technological era."

---

## SCENE CONTINUITY

Narration should flow through:

- connected locations
- sequential environments
- visual progression
- physical transitions

The engine encourages:

- cinematic continuity
- location persistence
- environmental anchoring
- coherent visual geography

## PACING PHILOSOPHY

Vidrush scripting should maintain:

- scene movement
- environmental variation
- cinematic progression
- visual rhythm

The engine discourages:

- endless talking-head narration
- abstract monologues
- information blocks without visuals
- long conceptual tangents

The layer supports:

- AI-generated cinematic footage

- hybrid documentary workflows

- stock footage
- generated visuals
- cinematic reconstruction
- documentary editing systems

## DOCUMENTARY VISUAL LANGUAGE

The engine encourages:

- industrial realism
- environmental storytelling
- physical systems
- grounded cinematics
- observable infrastructure

The engine discourages:

- generic cinematic filler
- random montage pacing
- visually disconnected narration

---

## RETRIEVAL FAILURE MODES

Retrieval failure looks like:

- abstract narration
- invisible concepts
- philosophical monologues
- non-visual scripting
- disconnected scene progression
- repetitive environments
- generic footage matching
- visual ambiguity

---

## OUTPUT STANDARDS

Outputs should produce narration that is:

- cinematic
- visually retrievable
- physically grounded
- scene-oriented
- environment-aware
- documentary-safe

The audience should feel like:

they are moving through real places and systems.

---

## ENFORCEMENT

The layer actively rejects:

- abstraction-heavy narration
- invisible storytelling
- motivational scripting
- generic AI documentary pacing
- footage-hostile narration
- retrieval collapse

Visual retrievability remains mandatory.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

If the audience cannot clearly visualize the narration,
Vidrush probably cannot retrieve it either.

# TOPIC_DISCOVERY_ENGINE

## PURPOSE

TOPIC_DISCOVERY_ENGINE defines how FacelessOS identifies strong documentary topics.

The engine exists to discover topics with:

- structural tension
- hidden systems
- strong narrative potential
- visual retrievability
- overlay compatibility
- long-form sustainability
- thumbnail/title potential

The engine prioritizes:

- documentary value
- systems intrigue
- consequence chains
- audience curiosity

over generic trend chasing.

---

## ACTIVATION

Activate TOPIC_DISCOVERY_ENGINE when:

- searching for new video ideas
- evaluating documentary potential
- scoring upload candidates
- researching trends
- analyzing channels
- generating title directions
- exploring audience curiosity patterns

## CORE PHILOSOPHY

Strong documentary topics are usually:

- system-driven
- consequence-heavy
- curiosity-rich
- visually grounded
- emotionally implied
- structurally revealing

The engine avoids:

- generic trending topics
- shallow internet drama
- low-consequence news
- disposable algorithm bait
- empty educational topics

The goal is discovering stories that feel:

important beneath the surface.

---

## DOCUMENTARY TOPIC PRINCIPLE

The strongest documentary topics usually contain:

- hidden mechanics
- unanswered questions
- systemic fragility
- institutional behavior
- consequence chains
- historical tension
- human stakes
- visual environments

Topics should naturally provoke:

"Wait… how does that actually work?"

or:

"I didn’t realize that mattered."

---

## TOPIC EVALUATION SYSTEM

The engine evaluates topics across:

### 1. SYSTEM DEPTH

Does the topic reveal:

- hidden systems
- structural mechanics
- institutional behavior
- infrastructure dependency
- technological consequence

---

### 2. NARRATIVE POTENTIAL

Does the topic support:

- escalation
- discovery
- tension progression
- consequence reveals
- cinematic structure

---

### 3. VISUAL RETRIEVABILITY

Can the topic produce:

- environments
- machinery
- maps
- interfaces
- physical locations
- cinematic imagery
- observable systems

especially for Vidrush workflows.

---

### 4. OVERLAY COMPATIBILITY

Does the topic naturally fit:

- Dead Game Archive
- Crude Lore
- Retirement Desk

without forcing identity mismatch?

---

### 5. TITLE + THUMBNAIL STRENGTH

Does the topic naturally create:

- curiosity
- investigative tension
- consequence framing
- visual intrigue
- strong packaging potential

WITHOUT requiring clickbait distortion?

---

### 6. LONGFORM SUSTAINABILITY

Can the topic support:

- layered explanation
- progressive reveals
- pacing variation
- multiple narrative stages

or is it too shallow?

---

## CHANNEL INTELLIGENCE PHILOSOPHY

The engine may eventually analyze:

- existing uploads
- audience behavior
- thumbnails
- retention patterns
- comments
- recurring themes
- successful pacing structures

to identify:

- identity-compatible topics
- audience expectation zones
- successful narrative patterns
- unexplored adjacent systems

The goal is:

editorial continuity,
not algorithmic cloning.

---

## TOPIC SOURCING

The engine favors sourcing from:

- historical systems
- infrastructure stories
- industrial processes
- preservation communities
- institutional failures
- overlooked economic systems
- hidden technology
- archival discoveries
- operational fragility
- long-tail curiosity subjects

The engine discourages:

- celebrity drama
- shallow outrage
- disposable trend cycles
- low-trust internet discourse

---

## TITLE DISCOVERY PRINCIPLE

Good documentary titles create:

- curiosity
- implication
- consequence
- hidden-system intrigue

The engine avoids:

- fake urgency
- exaggerated fear
- algorithmic screaming
- all-caps outrage framing

GOOD EXAMPLES:

- "The Server That Refused To Die"
- "The Pipeline Beneath Europe"
- "Why Retirees Are Quietly Leaving Florida"
- "How One Ship Stopped Global Trade"

BAD EXAMPLES:

- "THIS CHANGES EVERYTHING"
- "THE END IS HERE"
- "YOU NEED TO WATCH THIS NOW"

---

## THUMBNAIL DISCOVERY PRINCIPLE

Strong documentary packaging usually includes:

- isolated subjects
- environmental scale
- strong silhouettes
- readable typography
- restrained composition
- investigative tension

The engine discourages:

- clutter
- reaction faces
- arrow spam
- emotional overstatement
- meme aesthetics

---

## FAILURE MODES

Topic discovery failure looks like:

- generic trends
- shallow educational content
- weak narrative structure
- low visual potential
- weak overlays
- packaging dependence
- algorithm chasing
- identity drift

---

## OUTPUT STANDARDS

Outputs should produce topics that feel:

- cinematic
- investigative
- structurally interesting
- visually rich
- consequence-driven
- overlay-compatible
- documentary-worthy

The audience should feel:

they are uncovering something hidden beneath the surface.

---

## ENFORCEMENT

The engine actively rejects:

- low-trust clickbait
- outrage farming
- shallow trends
- disposable algorithm bait
- identity-breaking topics
- weak documentary structures

Narrative depth remains mandatory.

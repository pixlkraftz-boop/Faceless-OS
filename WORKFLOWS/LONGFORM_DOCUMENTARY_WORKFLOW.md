# LONGFORM_DOCUMENTARY_WORKFLOW

## PURPOSE

LONGFORM_DOCUMENTARY_WORKFLOW defines the standard orchestration sequence for long-form FacelessOS documentaries.

The workflow exists to preserve:

- narrative coherence
- pacing stability
- retrieval-safe scripting
- editorial continuity
- cinematic structure
- overlay consistency

across AI-assisted documentary production.

The workflow is designed primarily for:

- 8–20 minute documentaries
- Vidrush workflows
- voiceover-first production
- cinematic informational storytelling

---

## ACTIVATION

Activate this workflow when:

- producing long-form documentaries
- scripting investigative videos
- creating cinematic explainers
- building system-focused storytelling
- generating documentary voiceovers

Especially for:

- Dead Game Archive
- Crude Lore
- Retirement Desk

## WORKFLOW PHILOSOPHY

The workflow treats documentaries as:

- progressive discovery systems
- guided investigations
- cinematic information journeys
- consequence-reveal structures

The workflow prioritizes:

- clarity
- escalation discipline
- environmental grounding
- human pacing
- retrieval-safe narration

The workflow rejects:

- AI lecture pacing
- hyperactive scripting
- nonstop escalation
- abstraction-heavy narration
- algorithmic tension spam

---

# WORKFLOW SEQUENCE

## STAGE 1 — TOPIC IDENTIFICATION

Goal:
Find a topic with:

- structural tension
- hidden systems
- consequence chains
- strong documentary potential
- visual retrievability
- overlay compatibility

The workflow favors:

- overlooked systems
- forgotten histories
- institutional failures
- industrial dependencies
- abandoned infrastructure
- preservation stories

---

## STAGE 2 — THESIS FORMATION

Goal:
Define:

- what the documentary is actually about
- why it matters
- what hidden system is being revealed
- what emotional framing exists

The thesis should remain:

- clear
- grounded
- documentary-oriented
- consequence-aware

The workflow avoids:

- vague themes
- broad philosophical abstraction
- generic educational framing

---

## STAGE 3 — NARRATIVE ARC DESIGN

Goal:
Build the documentary progression.

Typical flow:

1. Orientation
2. Tension Introduction
3. System Expansion
4. Structural Revelation
5. Consequence Escalation
6. Human Impact
7. Final Perspective

The workflow maintains:

- pacing variation
- audience orientation
- progressive understanding

---

## STAGE 4 — VISUAL MAPPING

Goal:
Ensure narration remains visually retrievable.

Scripts should include:

- locations
- environments
- machinery
- systems
- actions
- physical progression
- cinematic continuity

The workflow actively checks for:

- abstraction overload
- invisible narration
- footage retrieval weakness

Especially for Vidrush workflows.

---

## STAGE 5 — HUMAN TEXTURE PASS

Goal:
Remove AI-feeling narration.

Check for:

- motivational cadence
- symmetrical phrasing
- emotional inflation
- repetitive sentence rhythm
- synthetic escalation

The narration should feel:

- human
- observational
- restrained
- believable

---

## STAGE 6 — PACING PASS

Goal:
Ensure pacing breathes naturally.

Balance:

- tension
- explanation
- reflection
- observation
- escalation
- visual movement

The workflow rejects:

- nonstop stimulation
- endless exposition
- tension stacking

---

## STAGE 7 — OVERLAY CALIBRATION

Goal:
Apply channel identity.

The overlay controls:

- emotional calibration
- visual philosophy
- pacing flavor
- audience psychology
- tonal identity

The workflow ensures:

the documentary feels native to the channel identity.

---

## STAGE 8 — TITLE + THUMBNAIL CALIBRATION

Goal:
Ensure packaging matches documentary tone.

Packaging should create:

- curiosity
- consequence
- investigative tension
- documentary intrigue

Avoid:

- rage bait
- fake urgency
- algorithmic sensationalism
- misleading framing

---

## STAGE 9 — FINAL RETRIEVAL CHECK

Goal:
Verify visual retrievability.

The workflow checks for:

- visual nouns
- observable environments
- scene continuity
- cinematic progression
- footage match quality

The documentary should remain compatible with:

- Vidrush
- stock footage systems
- AI-generated visuals
- hybrid documentary pipelines

---

## FAILURE MODES

Workflow failure looks like:

- lecture pacing
- AI narration feel
- abstraction-heavy scripting
- visual retrieval collapse
- hyperactive pacing
- identity drift
- emotional manipulation
- overlay inconsistency

---

## OUTPUT STANDARDS

Outputs should feel:

- cinematic
- investigative
- coherent
- visually grounded
- documentary-like
- consequence-driven
- human

The audience should feel guided through a real system.

Not processed through content automation.

---

## ENFORCEMENT

The workflow actively rejects:

- AI slop pacing
- motivational narration
- fake suspense
- hyperactive editing philosophy
- abstraction-heavy scripting
- algorithmic outrage framing

Editorial coherence remains mandatory.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

A documentary should feel like reality being uncovered slowly and clearly.

Not content being aggressively optimized.

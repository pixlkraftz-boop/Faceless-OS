# TOPIC_TO_SCRIPT

## PURPOSE

TOPIC_TO_SCRIPT defines the operational workflow for turning a documentary topic into a finished narration-ready script inside FacelessOS.

The workflow exists to preserve:

- strong packaging
- narrative coherence
- retrieval-safe scripting
- overlay identity
- pacing stability
- documentary realism

while accelerating production speed.

This workflow is designed specifically for:

- long-form documentaries
- Vidrush workflows
- cinematic explainers
- AI-assisted production

---

## ACTIVATION

Activate TOPIC_TO_SCRIPT when:

- generating documentary scripts
- developing new uploads
- turning ideas into productions
- building long-form narration
- scripting overlay-specific documentaries

# WORKFLOW SEQUENCE

## STAGE 1 — TOPIC VALIDATION

Goal:
Verify the topic has:

- documentary depth
- systems intrigue
- visual retrievability
- packaging potential
- overlay compatibility
- escalation potential

The workflow rejects:

- shallow trends
- weak visuals
- low consequence topics
- identity mismatch topics

---

## STAGE 2 — PACKAGING DISCOVERY

Before scripting begins:

define:
- title direction
- thumbnail tension
- curiosity framing
- emotional pressure
- viewer question

The packaging should create:

- investigative curiosity
- structural tension
- hidden-system intrigue

NOT:
- fake outrage
- exaggerated fear
- algorithmic screaming

Packaging defines:
the documentary’s narrative gravity.

---

## STAGE 3 — THESIS FORMATION

Define:

- what the documentary is actually revealing
- why the system matters
- where tension exists
- what hidden mechanism is being uncovered

The thesis should remain:

- specific
- consequence-driven
- documentary-oriented
- visually explorable

---

## STAGE 4 — HOOK GENERATION

The opening should:

- orient the audience quickly
- establish tension
- imply consequence
- create curiosity
- introduce the hidden system

The hook should NOT:

- over-explain
- lecture immediately
- scream importance
- rely on fake suspense

The audience should immediately feel:

"There’s something important beneath this."

---

## STAGE 5 — STRUCTURE MAPPING

Build the documentary structure.

Typical progression:

1. Orientation
2. Tension Introduction
3. System Expansion
4. Structural Revelation
5. Consequence Escalation
6. Human Impact
7. Final Perspective

The structure should maintain:

- pacing variation
- audience orientation
- escalating understanding
- visual progression

---

## STAGE 6 — VISUAL MAPPING

Every major section should contain:

- environments
- visual nouns
- observable actions
- cinematic transitions
- physical systems
- retrievable moments

Especially for:

- Vidrush
- stock retrieval
- AI-generated visuals
- documentary editing

The script should remain visually generatable.

---

## STAGE 7 — HUMAN TEXTURE PASS

Remove:

- AI cadence
- emotional inflation
- symmetrical phrasing
- motivational tone
- synthetic narration feel

Ensure narration feels:

- observational
- grounded
- restrained
- believable
- human

---

## STAGE 8 — OVERLAY CALIBRATION

Apply overlay identity.

The overlay determines:

- pacing flavor
- emotional calibration
- visual philosophy
- audience psychology
- tonal pressure

The documentary should feel native to the channel.

---

## STAGE 9 — RETENTION PASS

Check for:

- pacing stagnation
- excessive exposition
- weak transitions
- information overload
- visual repetition

The workflow encourages:

- escalating discovery
- environmental movement
- visual progression
- consequence reveals

Retention should emerge naturally from curiosity.

NOT from artificial stimulation.

---

## STAGE 10 — FINAL VIDRUSH PASS

Verify:

- visual retrievability
- footage compatibility
- scene continuity
- environmental grounding
- cinematic progression

The final script should remain highly compatible with:

- Vidrush
- stock systems
- AI-generated visuals
- hybrid documentary workflows

---

## FAILURE MODES

Workflow failure looks like:

- lecture pacing
- AI narration feel
- weak packaging
- retrieval collapse
- pacing exhaustion
- identity drift
- abstraction-heavy scripting
- low visual density

---

## OUTPUT STANDARDS

Outputs should produce scripts that feel:

- cinematic
- documentary-like
- visually grounded
- consequence-driven
- investigative
- human
- overlay-native

The audience should feel:

they are uncovering a real hidden system.

---

## ENFORCEMENT

The workflow actively rejects:

- AI slop scripting
- fake suspense
- hyperactive pacing
- motivational narration
- outrage optimization
- abstraction-heavy writing
- generic educational scripting

Narrative realism remains mandatory.

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

A documentary script should feel discovered.

Not manufactured.
